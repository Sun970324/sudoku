본 앱(이하 "앱")은 이용자의 개인정보를 중요하게 생각하며, 관련 법령을 준수합니다. 본 방침은 앱이 어떤 정보를 수집하고 어떻게 이용하는지 설명합니다.

1. 수집하는 정보

(1) 계정 정보
- Google 또는 Apple 계정으로 로그인하는 경우: 해당 서비스가 제공하는 계정 식별자, 이메일 주소
  · Apple 로그인에서 "이메일 가리기"를 선택하면 앱은 Apple이 발급한 전달용 주소(private relay)만 받으며, 실제 이메일 주소를 알 수 없습니다.
- 이메일로 가입하는 경우: 이메일 주소 (비밀번호는 앱이 보관하지 않으며, 인증 서비스가 단방향 암호화해 저장합니다)
- 게스트 계정: 로그인 화면의 "게스트로 시작"을 누르면 만들어지며, 기기당 한 번만 가능합니다. 익명 계정 식별자만 있고 이메일 등 개인 식별 정보는 없습니다. 나중에 Google·Apple 계정을 연동하거나 이메일 주소를 등록하면 기록을 유지한 채 정식 계정이 되며, 그때부터 위의 계정 정보를 수집합니다.
- 앱 내에서 설정한 닉네임

(2) 게임 기록
- 실시간 대결 기록(승패, 레이팅, 상대방, 대결 시각, 종료 사유)
- 시즌 종료 시점의 최종 순위·티어·레이팅 기록
- 데일리 스도쿠 완료 기록(완료 시각, 소요 시간, 실수 횟수, 힌트 사용 횟수)
- 혼자 하기 완료 시간은 개인 기록으로 저장하지 않습니다. 퍼즐을 완료하면 "이 난이도를 10초 단위 구간에서 몇 명이 완료했는지"를 세는 공용 집계표의 숫자만 1 올라갑니다(로그인 여부와 무관합니다). 이 표에는 이용자 식별자가 없어 특정 이용자의 기록을 되짚을 수 없으며, 상위 몇 %인지를 계산하고 분포를 보여주기 위해서만 쓰입니다.

- 서비스 개선을 위해 로그인한 계정의 주요 기능 사용 이벤트(첫 실행 시점, 가입, 첫 퍼즐 완료, 데일리 스도쿠 완료, 대결 로비 방문, 프리미엄 화면 열람)와 발생 시각을 수집합니다.

(7) 설치 식별자 (중복 로그인 방지용)
- 한 계정이 여러 기기에서 동시에 사용되는 것을 막기 위해, 앱이 설치될 때 생성하는 임의의 설치 식별자를 계정과 함께 저장합니다.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 11, 2026 (first issued July 26, 2026; usage records, installation identifier, purchase information and diagnostics added)

This app respects your privacy and complies with applicable laws. This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

(1) Account information
- When signing in with Google or Apple: the account identifier and email address provided by that service.
  · If you choose "Hide My Email" with Apple, the app only ever receives Apple's private relay address and never learns your real email address.
- When signing up with email: your email address. The app never stores your password; the authentication service keeps only a one-way hash of it.
- Guest accounts: created by "Continue as Guest" on the sign-in screen, which is available once per device. They hold an anonymous account identifier and no email or other identifying data. Linking a Google or Apple account later — or registering an email address — turns one into a full account while keeping your record, and the account information above is collected from that point on.
- The nickname you set in the app.

(2) Game records
- Real-time race records (result, rating, opponent, time of match, how it ended)
- Final rank, tier, and rating snapshots taken when a season ends
- Daily sudoku completion records (completion time, elapsed time, mistakes, hints used)
- Solo finish times are not stored as personal records. Completing a puzzle — signed in or not — increments one number in a shared tally of how many players finished that difficulty within each 10-second band. That tally holds no user identifier, so no finish can be traced back to anyone; it exists only to work out percentile standings and to show the distribution.

(3) Reports
- When you report another player we store the account identifiers of the reporter and the reported player, the reason, any details you type, and the related race identifier. This is used only to review the report and decide on enforcement, and is never shown to other players.

(4) Advertising
- The app shows rewarded ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information such as advertising identifiers (ADID/IDFA) to serve ads. See Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details.
- Whether ads are personalized is your choice, made in the consent dialog (Google User Messaging Platform) shown on first launch. On iOS you can change it at any time under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking; on Android under Settings > Google > Ads.

(5) On-device data
- Game progress, per-difficulty statistics, replays, favorites, and settings (theme, language, sound, etc.) are stored only on your device and are never sent to our servers.

(6) Usage records
- To improve the service we record, for signed-in accounts, when a few key features are used (first launch, sign-up, first puzzle completed, daily sudoku completed, race lobby visited, premium page opened) and when each happened.
- These usage records contain no advertising identifier and no hardware information about your device; they are tied only to your account identifier. Nothing is recorded while you are not signed in.

(7) Installation identifier (for one-device sign-in)
- To stop one account being used on several devices at once, a random identifier created when the app is installed is stored alongside your account.
- It is not your device's hardware or advertising identifier: it names "this installation of the app" and nothing else, and reinstalling the app issues a new one. Only the most recently signed-in installation is kept.

(8) Purchase information
- When you buy premium, the product identifier, the store (App Store / Google Play), the subscription expiry date, and the transaction identifier issued by the store are stored with your account. This is how a purchase is confirmed and restored when you change devices.
- Neither the app nor our servers receive or store payment details such as card numbers; the payment itself is handled by the App Store or Google Play.
- If receipt verification fails, the reason and the product/store involved may be recorded so the cause can be found.

(9) Diagnostics
- When the app crashes or hits an error, Google Firebase Crashlytics collects where the error occurred, your device model and OS version, and an installation identifier issued by Firebase. This is used to find and fix the cause; it does not include anything you typed or your puzzle records.
- This is collected only in publicly released builds.

The app does not request location permission, and does not access your contacts, photos, microphone, or camera. Google AdMob, which serves the ads, may infer an approximate region from information such as your IP address in order to serve them. The app never uses your device's location services to determine where you are.

2. How We Use Information
- Account management and sign-in
- Race matchmaking, rating/tier calculation, and race history
- Daily sudoku and rating leaderboards
- Handling reports and responding to abuse
- Serving ads
- Analyzing usage statistics to improve the service
- Preventing one account from being used on several devices at once
- Confirming premium purchases and restoring entitlements
- Diagnosing errors and improving app stability

3. Retention and Deletion
- Account information and game records are kept while your account exists.
- When you delete your account, your sign-in methods (Google/Apple links and sessions) and email address are destroyed immediately, and your nickname is replaced with "Deleted user" so you can no longer be identified.
- Race records themselves are not deleted; they remain in anonymized form. A race record is also your opponent's win/loss and rating history, so erasing it would damage another player's record.
- Reports are kept for up to one year after they are resolved, then deleted.
- For 7 days after deletion, the same account cannot sign up again. To check this, only a one-way hash of your sign-in identifiers (email address and social sign-in id) is kept for those 7 days and then destroyed automatically. A hash cannot be turned back into the original value; it is used solely to tell whether a new sign-up is the same account, and is linked to no record or identity.
- Usage records are deleted automatically 13 months after collection, and immediately when you delete your account.
- The installation identifier and purchase information are deleted immediately when you delete your account.
- Diagnostics are retained for up to 90 days under Google Firebase Crashlytics' own policy, then deleted.
- On-device data is removed when you uninstall the app.
