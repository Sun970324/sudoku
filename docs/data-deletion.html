Account and Data Deletion

App: Sudoku League (스도쿠 리그)

■ How to delete your account

Option 1. In the app (recommended)
1. Open the app and tap the person icon at the top right of the home screen.
2. On My Page, tap "Delete Account" near the bottom.
3. Confirm. The deletion is processed immediately.

Option 2. By email
If you have already uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, write to
contact@jaywithme.com and we will process the request after verifying your identity.

■ Data deleted (immediately on request)

- Your Google or Apple account link and account identifier
- Email address
- Sign-in sessions (signed out on every device)
- Nickname — replaced with "Deleted user", leaving nothing identifying
- Profile image
- Sign in with Apple tokens — revoked with Apple as part of the deletion
- Pending matchmaking entries and unused race room codes
- XP, your level, and the most recent XP award record of each kind (solo, daily,
  ranked)
- Solo records (games, perfect games, best time and average per difficulty)
- Titles you have earned, your equipped title and nickname color, and your
  game count for each technique family
- Completion effects you have earned and the one you have equipped
- Event nickname colors you have earned
- Per-run XP totals of XP events and the identifier of the last game counted
- On-device data (game progress, statistics, settings) is removed when you
  uninstall the app

■ Data retained, and why

- Race records (results, rating changes, timestamps) and daily sudoku records
  are kept in anonymized form rather than deleted. A race record is also the
  opponent's history and rating trail, so erasing it would damage another
  player's record. In these records you appear only as "Deleted user", with
  nothing that identifies you.
- Reports you filed are kept for up to one year after they are resolved, for
  review and abuse prevention, and then destroyed.
- A one-way hash of your sign-in identifiers (email address and social sign-in
  id) is kept for 7 days. It exists only to enforce the re-registration wait
  below: a hash cannot be turned back into the original value, and is used
  solely to tell whether a new sign-up is the same account. It is destroyed
  automatically after 7 days and is linked to no record or identity.

■ Signing up again

For 7 days after you delete your account, neither the same Google or Apple
account nor the same email address can be used to sign up again. This stops
deletion and re-registration from being used to undo a rating or walk away
from a report. After 7 days you can sign up again with no further steps.

■ Deleting some data while keeping your account

If you want specific information erased without closing your account, write to
contact@jaywithme.com and say what you would like removed; we will handle it after
confirming the request. Your nickname can be changed at any time from My Page
in the app.

Race and daily sudoku records are the exception. A race record is also the
opponent's history and rating trail, so removing it would damage another
player's record. Those records hold nothing that identifies you.

■ Retention periods

Anonymized race and daily records carry nothing that identifies a person and
may be kept for as long as the service runs. Their move-by-move records
(replays) are the exception: those are destroyed 30 days after collection
whether or not the account still exists. Reports are kept for at most one
year, and the re-registration hash for 7 days. Images uploaded by sharing are
not stored against your account, so they stay until 30 days after upload, even
after the account is deleted, and are then deleted automatically. Everything
else listed above is
destroyed immediately on request, with no grace period.

  保管します。これは下記の“再登録の制限”を確認するためのものであり、ハッシュ値は元の値に
  戻すことができず、新規登録が同一のアカウントによるものかを照合する目的にのみ使用されます。7日が
  経過すると自動的に廃棄され、利用者の記録や身元とは紐付けられません。

■ 再登録の制限

アカウントを削除した後7日間は、同じGoogle・Appleアカウントまたは同じメールアドレスで再度
登録することはできません。削除と再登録を繰り返してレーティングを元に戻したり、通報を回避したりする
ことを防ぐための措置です。7日が経過すると、特別な手続きなしに再度登録することが
できます。

ニックネームは、アプリ内のマイページからいつでもご自身で変更できます。

ただし、対戦記録および今日のナンプレの記録は削除いたしかねます。1件の対戦
記録は対戦相手の戦績であり、レーティング履歴でもあるため、消去すると他の利用者の記録も
あわせて損なわれるためです。これらの記録には、個人を識別できる情報は残り
ません。

■ 保管期間

匿名化された対戦・今日のナンプレの記録は個人を識別できない状態であるため、サービスが
継続する間保管される場合があります。ただし、その対局の手順記録(リプレイ)は例外であり、

共有画像はアカウントと紐付けられていないため、アカウントを削除しても、アップロード日から30日が
経過するまで残り、その後自動的に削除されます。その他の個人情報は
上記の一覧のとおり、ご依頼後直ちに廃棄され、別途の猶予期間はありません。

重新註冊。此措施旨在防止透過反覆刪除及重新註冊來回復積分或規避檢舉。
7 天期滿後，無須任何額外程序即可重新
