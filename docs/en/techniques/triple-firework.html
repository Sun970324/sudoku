Triple Firework

Firework

A Triple Firework is a cell in a box plus one cell in the same row and one in the same column outside the box, which must share three digits. Every other candidate can be removed from those three cells.

Cells needed
One in the box, one in its row, one in its column
Digits
Three
What to remove
Other candidates in the three cells

The Triple Firework pattern

The cells are R2C2 in box 1, R2C7 in the same row outside the box and R8C2 in the same column outside the box. Outside the box, row 2 has 1, 5 and 8 only in R2C7, and column 2 has them only in R8C2.

1, 5 and 8, shared by the three cells Candidates to remove Candidates in other cells

Why does this work?

Take the digit 1 (5 and 8 work the same way). Suppose 1 is in neither R2C7 nor R8C2.

Then row 2's 1Its only candidate outside the box is R2C7, so it goes in the row 2 part of the box.
And column 2's 1Its only candidate outside the box is R8C2, so it goes in the column 2 part of the box.

The box holds only one 1, so both must be the same cell, the one in row 2 and column 2: R2C2. So 1 is always in one of the three cells. The same goes for 5 and 8. Three digits fill the three cells one each, leaving no room for anything else. Remove the 3 from R2C2, the 6 from R2C7 and the 9 from R8C2.

How to find the Triple Firework

  1. Pick a box and a cell in it where a row and a column cross.
  2. Find three digits that have only one candidate in that row outside the box and one in that column outside the box, the same two cells for all three digits.
  3. Remove every candidate that is not one of the three digits from the three cells.

Common questions

Why "firework"?

The pattern bursts from one cell along its row and its column, like a firework.

What about two digits?

A two-digit version exists, but it needs extra conditions to reach a conclusion. Sudoku League uses the three-digit pattern.

May the digit appear elsewhere in the box?

Yes. It can be in the row and column cells inside the box. Only outside the box does each line need a single candidate.
