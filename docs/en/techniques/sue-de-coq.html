Sue de Coq

Sue de Coq is when the candidates of cells where a box and a line overlap are split between a cell on the line and a cell in the box. Each side's digits can then be removed from the rest of the line and the rest of the box.

Cells needed
Two overlap cells, one on the line, one in the box
Candidates
Four digits in all
What to remove
The rest of the line and the box

The Sue de Coq pattern

Where row 1 and box 1 overlap sit R1C1 (1, 2, 4, 5) and R1C2 (1, 4, 5). On the row side is R1C7 (1, 2), and on the box side R3C3 (4, 5). Together the four cells hold 1, 2, 4 and 5, with 1 and 2 on the row side and 4 and 5 on the box side.

The two overlap cells The row cell (1 and 2) The box cell (4 and 5) Candidates to remove

Why does this work?

All candidates of the four cells (the two overlap cells, R1C7 and R3C3) are within 1, 2, 4 and 5. The overlap cells share a row with the row cell and a box with the box cell, so they cannot repeat a digit of either. The row side (1, 2) and the box side (4, 5) have no digit in common, so the four cells take 1, 2, 4 and 5 once each.

1 and 2 are therefore inside row 1, in the overlap cells or R1C7, so no other cell of row 1 can hold them. 4 and 5 are inside box 1, in the overlap cells or R3C3, so no other cell of the box can hold them. That removes the 1 from R1C5 and the 5 from R2C3.

How to find the Sue de Coq

  1. Where a box and a line overlap, find two cells with four digits between them (or three cells with five).
  2. On the line, find a cell with two of those digits, and in the box a cell with the other two. The two sides must not share a digit.
  3. Remove the line side's digits from the rest of the line and the box side's digits from the rest of the box.

Common questions

Where does the name come from?

It comes from the nickname of the person who first published it. The name stuck even though it says nothing about the pattern.

Can the overlap have three cells?

Yes. Three cells hold five digits, and the line and box sides split the rest.

What if the line and box cells share a digit?

Then it does not work. The two sides must cover different digits.
