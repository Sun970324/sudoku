How to play sudoku

In classic sudoku you fill the empty cells of the 9×9 grid with the digits 1 to 9. Every row, every column and every 3×3 box marked off by the thick lines must hold each digit exactly once. The digits given at the start cannot be changed, and every puzzle has exactly one solution.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. You can play right in your browser, with no install and no sign-in.

Is this classic sudoku?

Yes. It is the standard 9×9 puzzle with the digits 1 to 9, with no variant rules like diagonals or killer cages.

What if I close the window mid-game?

Your game is saved in this browser, so you can pick up where you left off when you come back.

Which level should I start with?

If you are new, start with Easy. Easy needs only singles, and Pointing and pairs come in from Medium.

Does a hint just give me the answer?

No. It tells you in three steps: the technique's name, where it applies, then the answer. You get 3 hints per puzzle.

How many mistakes can I make?

Three. The third mistake ends the game, and you can replay the same puzzle or start a new one.

What else is in the app?

Live ranked races, races with up to 8 friends, Daily Sudoku, Replay, and every technique lesson.
